# tr-fr: 50 words to proofread (A1, spread evenly over 690 written words)

For each card check: the meaning, the gender and kind, that the sentences are natural, that every respelling reads like the target language when read with the letters of the native language, and the translations.

## 1. merhaba (interjection) [A1]

- Anlam: bonjour; salut
- Eş anlamlı: selam
- Merhaba, nasılsınız? — okunuş: *mèrhaba, nasîlsînîz?* — Bonjour, comment allez-vous ?
- Merhaba, ben Ali. — okunuş: *mèrhaba, bène ali.* — Salut, je suis Ali.
- Kalıp: **merhaba demek** (*mèrhaba démék*) = dire bonjour; Komşuya merhaba dedim. — *komchouya mèrhaba dédim.* — J'ai dit bonjour au voisin.
