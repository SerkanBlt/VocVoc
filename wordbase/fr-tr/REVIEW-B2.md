# fr-tr: 50 words to proofread (B2, spread evenly over 2047 written words)

For each card check: the meaning, the gender and kind, that the sentences are natural, that every respelling reads like the French when read with Turkish letters, and the translations.

## 1. meurtre (isim (eril)) [B2]

- Anlam: cinayet
- Eş anlamlı: homicide, assassinat
- La police enquête sur un meurtre. — okunuş: *la polis anket sür en mörtr* — Polis bir cinayeti soruşturuyor.
