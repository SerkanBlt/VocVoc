# fr-tr: 50 words to proofread (C1, spread evenly over 2500 written words)

For each card check: the meaning, the gender and kind, that the sentences are natural, that every respelling reads like the French when read with Turkish letters, and the translations.

## 1. seigneur (isim (eril)) [C1]

- Anlam: derebeyi; asil bey
- Eş anlamlı: maître, noble
- Zıt anlamlı: serf, vassal
- Le seigneur possède des terres immenses. — okunuş: *lö senyör poses de ter imans* — Derebeyi geniş topraklara sahip.
- Les paysans servent leur seigneur. — okunuş: *le peizan serv lör senyör* — Köylüler derebeylerine hizmet ediyor.
